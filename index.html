About This Data

  • ED visit rates are calculated weekly from local emergency department syndromic surveillance and reported as a rate to allow comparison across time periods.
  • Wastewater surveillance reflects viral genetic material measured in sewage samples, an early indicator of community transmission that is independent of testing or healthcare-seeking behavior.
  • Data are refreshed periodically; see the "Data updated" badge above for the most recent refresh date.
  • This dashboard is for general public health situational awareness and should not be used for individual medical decision-making.
