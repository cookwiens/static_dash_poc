Suicide Rate per 100,000

18.0 per 100,000 ▲ 2024

Suicide continues to be a pressing public health problem in Douglas County and the impact of these deaths on families and communities is far-reaching. Decreasing the suicide mortality rate is one of the objectives in the Douglas County Community Health and Improvement Plan (CHIP).

About this indicator

The unadjusted (or crude) suicide mortality rate is the number of deaths from suicide in Douglas County each year, divided by the county's total population. This rate is not ideal for comparing with other counties.

Unadjusted Suicide Mortality Rate = (Number of Suicide Deaths / Population) × 100,000
